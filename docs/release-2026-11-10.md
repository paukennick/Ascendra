## Settings that matter after this release

- `SIMS_ENABLED=true` in production, so sims are visible.
- `AI_ACCESS_MODE=granted` and `AI_GLOBAL_DAILY_CEILING_USD=5`: full AI
  only for owners and the people in Settings > Access, and a $5 daily
  ceiling across everyone.
- `BILLING_ENABLED` is off: no plans, prices or upgrade prompts.
- `SERVICE_EMAILS_ENABLED` is not set: reminders on the phone work, the
  service emails send nothing.

These values were reported by Nick; this page did not read them from Vercel.
